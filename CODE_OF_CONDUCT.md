# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Everyone participating in Tokenlens issues, pull requests, reviews, and official
project spaces is expected to be respectful and constructive. Welcome people
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Critique ideas and
code rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and respect others' time
and boundaries. This also applies when representing the project publicly.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam,
and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable. Do not publish another person's
private information or pressure volunteers for responses or unpaid work.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project maintainer, [@Kameleon21](https://github.com/Kameleon21), handles
moderation. To request a private contact channel, open an issue titled
"Private conduct contact requested" with no names, incident details, or personal
information. Wait for a private contact method before sharing details. If even
requesting contact publicly would be unsafe, or the complaint concerns the
maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines#reporting-an-issue-with-content-on-github)
for behavior that violates GitHub's policies. GitHub's process is independent of
project moderation. Do not use security advisories for conduct reports.

Reports will be handled discreetly; information may need to be shared where
necessary to investigate or comply with platform requirements. The maintainer
may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, issue a warning, or impose
a temporary or permanent participation ban. Responses should reflect the
severity, impact, and repetition of the behavior; serious threats or harassment
may warrant an immediate ban. When practical, explain the decision and allow a
private request for reconsideration with new context. Retaliation against a
reporter is unacceptable.

Moderation is best effort; there is no guaranteed response time. This project
policy supplements GitHub's own community guidelines.
