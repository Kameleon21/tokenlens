# Project governance

Tokenlens is maintained by [@Kameleon21](https://github.com/Kameleon21), who makes
final decisions about scope, architecture, releases, access, and moderation.
Contributors are welcome to propose changes and explain tradeoffs in issues and
pull requests. Decisions consider usefulness, correctness, privacy, compatibility,
and the cost of maintaining the change.

Discuss substantial features and dependencies before implementing them. When
there is disagreement, summarize the alternatives and evidence in the relevant
issue or PR. The maintainer will explain a decision where practical and may close
proposals that do not fit the project. New evidence can justify reconsideration;
repeated pressure does not override a decision.

Contributions do not automatically grant write access or decision-making rights.
Additional maintainers may be invited after sustained, reliable contributions;
access should be limited to what their responsibilities require. Update this file
and CODEOWNERS when maintainership changes. Only maintainers publish releases.

Maintenance is voluntary and best effort. Features may be deprecated and the
project may be paused or archived. Where practical, significant changes are
announced in the changelog or an issue. The MIT license permits independent forks.
